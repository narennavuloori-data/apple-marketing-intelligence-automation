# Apple Marketing Intelligence Automation — KPI Dictionary

This document explains the key business metrics used in the Apple Marketing Intelligence Automation project.

---

## 1. Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

**Definition:**  
Average marketing cost required to acquire one new customer.

**Formula:**  
`Total Marketing Cost / New Customers`

**Business Use:**  
Measures acquisition efficiency. Lower CAC usually indicates more cost-efficient marketing.

---

## 2. Lifetime Value (LTV)

**Definition:**  
Average net revenue or profit expected from a customer over their relationship with the business.

**Formula:**  
Common business formula:  
`Average Revenue Per Customer × Average Customer Lifespan`

**Business Use:**  
Helps assess the long-term value generated by acquired customers.

---

## 3. LTV:CAC Ratio

**Definition:**  
Relationship between customer lifetime value and acquisition cost.

**Formula:**  
`LTV / CAC`

**Business Use:**  
Shows whether customer acquisition is financially sustainable. A common benchmark is **3:1**.

---

## 4. Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)

**Definition:**  
Revenue generated for every dollar of ad spend.

**Formula:**  
`Attributed Revenue / Ad Spend`

**Business Use:**  
Measures the direct effectiveness of advertising spend.

---

## 5. Marketing Return on Investment (Marketing ROI)

**Definition:**  
Profitability of total marketing investment.

**Formula:**  
`(Gross Profit - Total Marketing Cost) / Total Marketing Cost × 100`

**Business Use:**  
Evaluates whether marketing is generating profitable business outcomes, not just sales.

---

## 6. Click-Through Rate (CTR)

**Definition:**  
Percentage of impressions that resulted in clicks.

**Formula:**  
`Clicks / Impressions × 100`

**Business Use:**  
Measures how attractive and relevant a campaign or creative is to the audience.

---

## 7. Cost Per Click (CPC)

**Definition:**  
Average advertising cost for each click.

**Formula:**  
`Ad Spend / Clicks`

**Business Use:**  
Helps evaluate traffic acquisition efficiency.

---

## 8. Cost Per Mille (CPM)

**Definition:**  
Cost to generate one thousand impressions.

**Formula:**  
`Ad Spend / Impressions × 1000`

**Business Use:**  
Useful for measuring awareness-campaign cost efficiency.

---

## 9. Cost Per Acquisition (CPA)

**Definition:**  
Average advertising cost per conversion or acquisition.

**Formula:**  
`Ad Spend / Conversions`

**Business Use:**  
Shows how much it costs to drive each completed conversion.

---

## 10. Conversion Rate

**Definition:**  
Percentage of clicks that convert into the desired action.

**Formula:**  
`Conversions / Clicks × 100`

**Business Use:**  
Measures landing page and campaign effectiveness in converting traffic.

---

## 11. Average Order Value (AOV)

**Definition:**  
Average revenue generated per order.

**Formula:**  
`Total Net Revenue / Total Orders`

**Business Use:**  
Helps understand transaction size and customer purchase value.

---

## 12. Retention Rate

**Definition:**  
Percentage of customers retained over a given period.

**Formula:**  
Common business formula:  
`(Customers at End of Period - New Customers During Period) / Customers at Start of Period × 100`

**Business Use:**  
Measures customer loyalty and the ability to keep customers over time.

---

## 13. Churn Rate

**Definition:**  
Percentage of customers lost during a period.

**Formula:**  
`Churned Customers / Customers at Start of Period × 100`

**Business Use:**  
Measures customer attrition and highlights retention challenges.

---

## 14. Repeat Purchase Rate

**Definition:**  
Percentage of customers who purchased more than once.

**Formula:**  
`Customers with More Than One Order / Total Purchasing Customers × 100`

**Business Use:**  
Shows how well the business drives repeat buying behavior.

---

## 15. Gross Margin

**Definition:**  
Percentage of revenue remaining after cost of goods sold.

**Formula:**  
`Gross Profit / Net Revenue × 100`

**Business Use:**  
Measures product-level or business-level profitability before operating expenses.

---

## 16. Supporting Cost Definition — Total Marketing Cost

**Definition:**  
Combined marketing cost used in CAC and Marketing ROI.

**Formula:**  
`Ad Spend + Creative Cost + Agency Cost + Marketing Tools Cost`

**Business Use:**  
Provides a more complete measure of marketing investment than ad spend alone.

---

## 17. KPI Interpretation Notes

- **Higher is generally better:** ROAS, Marketing ROI, CTR, Conversion Rate, AOV, Retention Rate, Repeat Purchase Rate, Gross Margin, LTV, LTV:CAC
- **Lower is generally better:** CAC, CPC, CPM, CPA, Churn Rate
- **Use context carefully:** A high CTR is good, but it must also lead to conversions and profitable revenue.
- **Use multiple KPIs together:** For example, strong ROAS with low gross margin may still be less attractive than a slightly lower ROAS with stronger profitability.
